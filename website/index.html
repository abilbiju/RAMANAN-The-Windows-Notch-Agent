Features

Everything lives in the notch.

Glance up for what matters, hover for more, and forget it's there the rest of the time.

Now playing, everywhere

Spotify, YouTube in your browser, Media Player, VLC: if it plays, the notch shows it. Album art, a seek bar, ±15 seconds, synced lyrics, and a one-click switch between speakers and headphones.

Changa Chaka Chakaachanga chaka chakaa ♪

System HUDs

Volume, brightness, Caps Lock, Num Lock and Do Not Disturb, in a notch that grows and shrinks with a little spring.

Battery that cares

A charging animation when you plug in, a heads-up when you unplug, and a nudge before you run low.

Privacy dots

A green dot when something's using your camera, an orange one for the mic, and the name of the app doing it.

Live downloads

Browser downloads show size and speed as they come in, then a tap on "Show" takes you to the file.

File shelf

Drop files on the notch to park them, then drag them out into an email, a chat or a folder.

Mirror

Check your hair before the call. The camera only switches on while the Mirror tab is open.

Put it anywhere

Drag the notch to any screen edge to dock it there, or drop it mid-screen as a floating pill.

A resident mascot

Pixel Ramanan rests in your notch while fish swim by, busts out bhangra when the music starts, and reacts to what your PC is up to. Or switch him off.

Make it yours

Pill, slim bar or invisible. Any size, any display, colours that match your album art or a colour you pick. Choose which alerts you want, lock its position, and start it with Windows.

Meet Ramanan

Your notch has a resident now.

He's a 16×19-pixel fan tribute to the legendary Ramanan from Punjabi House: curly hair, big moustache, bigger grin, yellow shirt, arms crossed like he owns the place.

  • Stands in your notch while pixel fish swim by
  • Blinks and raises an eyebrow when you least expect it
  • Dances to whatever you're playing, with real moves: bhangra, disco, the arm wave, a flex and more
  • Has moods: naps when you step away, sweats on low battery, parties when a download finishes and gasps when your camera turns on
  • Prefer a plain notch? Settings → Appearance → Mascot → None

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Ramanan is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts or upsells.

Why does Windows say it "protected my PC"?

SmartScreen warns about any app that isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate and that it hasn't seen many times yet. Click More info → Run anyway. If you'd rather check first, all of the source code is on GitHub and you can build the exe yourself.

What does it do with my data?

Nothing leaves your PC except lyrics lookups: the song title, artist and album go to LRCLIB to fetch synced lyrics, and you can turn that off in Settings. Every few hours it also asks GitHub whether a newer release exists, which sends nothing about you and can be turned off in Settings → About. There's no tracking or telemetry. The camera only runs while the Mirror tab is open.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No, Ramanan is Windows-only (10 and 11). It reads Windows' own media, audio, battery and privacy information.

Can I move the notch or hide it?

Drag it to any edge, or into the middle of the screen as a floating pill. You can lock its position, make it a slim bar or invisible until you hover, and it hides itself in fullscreen games and videos.

How do I uninstall it?

Turn off Start with Windows in Settings, right-click the tray icon and choose Quit, then delete Ramanan.exe. To remove its saved settings too, delete the .winnotch folder in your user folder.

Who is Ramanan?

Ramanan is the much-loved character from the Malayalam film Punjabi House (2002). The pixel mascot is an original fan tribute drawn in code. This project isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the film's makers or cast.
